# Calendar and service pages — 21 September 2026

Live admin: https://spotsup.ru/admin/#calendar . Administrator and station employee use their existing logins. The calendar offers day/week views and an equipment filter. Confirmed requests reserve physical units over their complete interval. New requests and season-pass purchases do not reserve stock. BIG SUP participant count reserves one physical board.

Confirmation, issue and reschedule capacity checks run inside SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE transactions. Adjacent intervals are allowed. Capacity uses peak simultaneous occupancy, not the sum of all overlapping bookings. Takeaway reservations include the return date until station closing. Day rentals end at station closing. Linked issues replace the reservation rather than counting twice. Existing walk-in/issued rentals have expected_return; overdue rentals conservatively block future reservations until returned. Historical rentals missing this field receive a one-hour estimate during additive migration. Staff should review any old active rentals.

Issue form requires planned return. Future bookings can prevent a late-start rental from running into the next reservation. Staff can shorten the planned return and enter the actually collected price, or reschedule after agreement. There is no automatic cancellation of late clients: a highlighted no-show becomes cancellable after the configured grace period. Cancellation releases stock and cancels queued reminders. Actual amounts and payment dates remain unchanged on return.

Calendar APIs require the same authenticated session as the existing admin. Capacity is not exposed with customer details to the public. SMS transport, provider credentials, consent, deduplication, daily limit and discount flow were preserved. No real customer SMS was generated by these tests.
